Food Tips for the Philippines

  • Try local dishes like Adobo, Sinigang, Lechon, and Halo-halo when possible.
  • Most restaurants accept credit cards, but it's good to have cash for small establishments.
  • Typical meal costs range from ₱100-250 ($2-4.50 USD) for fast food to ₱500-1,500 ($9-27 USD) for nicer restaurants.
  • Water is usually served free at restaurants, but always check if it's purified water.
  • Tipping is not required but appreciated (10% is generous).
